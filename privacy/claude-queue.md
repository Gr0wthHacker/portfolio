# Queue for Claude Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

Unofficial. Not affiliated with Anthropic.

## The short version

Queue for Claude has no servers, no analytics, and no accounts. It stores your queue and settings in your browser and sends nothing to the developer.

## What the extension does

Queue for Claude runs only on claude.ai and claude.com. It lets you queue messages for a Claude chat. When Claude finishes responding, the extension puts the next queued message into Claude's message box and clicks Send for you, just as if you had typed and sent it yourself. Those messages go to Claude the same way any message you send on claude.ai does. Queue for Claude doesn't send them anywhere else.

## Data stored on your device

The extension keeps the following in your browser's local extension storage (chrome.storage.local):

- The text of messages you have queued and not yet sent
- Your settings: queue on or off, auto-send on or off, and whether the panel is collapsed
- A status record of whether the extension can still find Claude's message box, send button, and stop button, used to show an error if Claude's page changes

This data stays on your device. It is removed when you clear the queue, send the queued messages, or uninstall the extension.

## Data collection and sharing

Queue for Claude does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any user data. The extension makes no network requests of its own and does not communicate with any service run by the developer or any third party.

## How permissions are used

- **storage**: to save your queued messages and settings locally on your device.
- **Access to claude.ai and claude.com**: to show the queue panel on those pages, read what you type in Claude's message box when you choose to queue it, insert your queued messages, click Send, and detect when Claude is responding.
